Meet Anthony Marshall

Hi, I'm Anthony Marshall, the founder and licensed agent behind MegaSure Insurance. I've been licensed to sell insurance in the state of Tennessee since 2018, and I built MegaSure to give families and individuals across Middle Tennessee a straightforward way to find health, life, dental, vision, and hospital indemnity coverage that actually fits their budget.

I live right here in Smyrna, Tennessee, and I work directly with my clients from the first quote through claims support. When you reach out to MegaSure, you're not routed to a call center or a different rep every time — you're working with me.

As an independent agent, I'm not tied to a single insurance company. That means I can shop coverage across multiple carriers to find the plan that fits your health needs and your budget, then walk you through the details in plain English before you ever sign anything.
