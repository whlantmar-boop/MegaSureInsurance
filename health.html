Tennessee Health Insurance FAQ

Get answers to common questions about health insurance in Tennessee.

How much does health insurance cost in Tennessee 2026?

Health insurance costs in Tennessee vary based on your age, location, and plan type. Individual plans typically range from $200-$600 per month before subsidies. Many Tennessee residents qualify for premium tax credits that can significantly reduce monthly costs. Use our instant quote tool to see your specific pricing and available subsidies.

What health insurance companies operate in Tennessee?

Major health insurance providers in Tennessee include BlueCross BlueShield of Tennessee, Ambetter (Celtic Insurance), Cigna Healthcare, and Oscar Health. Each offers different plan types, provider networks, and coverage areas across the state's 95 counties.

Can I get affordable health insurance in Tennessee if I'm self-employed?

Yes! Self-employed individuals in Tennessee can purchase health insurance through the federal marketplace and may qualify for premium tax credits based on income. Short-term health insurance and health sharing plans are also options, though they provide different levels of coverage than ACA-compliant plans.

Does Tennessee health insurance cover mental health and prescription drugs?

All ACA-compliant health insurance plans in Tennessee must cover essential health benefits including mental health services, substance abuse treatment, and prescription drugs. Coverage levels vary by plan, with preventive mental health services often covered at 100%.

How do I qualify for health insurance subsidies in Tennessee?

Tennessee residents can qualify for premium tax credits if their household income is between 100% and 400% of the Federal Poverty Level. For 2026, this means roughly $15,060 to $60,240 for individuals or $31,200 to $124,800 for a family of four. Cost-sharing reductions are available for those earning up to 250% of the poverty level.

What's the difference between health insurance in Nashville vs Memphis vs Knoxville?

While all Tennessee cities use the same federal marketplace, provider networks and hospital systems vary by region. Nashville has Vanderbilt and HCA networks, Memphis features Methodist Le Bonheur and Regional One Health, while Knoxville includes UT Medical Center and Covenant Health. Plan availability and pricing can differ slightly between regions.

When can I enroll in health insurance in Tennessee?

Open enrollment for Tennessee health insurance typically runs from November 1 through January 15 each year. You can also enroll during special enrollment periods if you experience qualifying life events like losing other coverage, getting married, having a baby, or moving to Tennessee.
