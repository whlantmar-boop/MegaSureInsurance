How does health insurance work for self-employed people in Tennessee?

A plain answer from Anthony Marshall, a licensed Tennessee insurance agent. Updated November 2026.

If you work for yourself, you buy a plan on your own, usually through HealthCare.gov. Your premium tax credit is based on your expected net income for the year, which is what you earn after business expenses, not your total sales. That estimate matters, because a lower income can mean a bigger credit. Be honest with it, and update it if your year changes. If you end up earning more than you estimated, you may owe some of the credit back at tax time. Many self-employed people can also deduct their premiums on their tax return, so ask your tax preparer how that works alongside the credit. If your business has employees, a small group plan may be another option.
