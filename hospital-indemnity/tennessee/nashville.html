Protecting Your Finances: Filling the Gaps in Your Health Insurance

Even with a solid health insurance plan, an unexpected hospital stay in Nashville can quickly lead to major financial stress. Between high out-of-pocket deductibles and lost work time, the expenses pile up fast.

The Reality of Nashville Healthcare Costs

High Deductibles

Many Nashville employers offer high-deductible health plans (HDHPs) that leave individuals and families with $3,000–$8,000 in out-of-pocket exposure before major medical coverage kicks in.

Staggering Costs

A typical hospital stay in Davidson County averages $12,500 or more, while ICU stays at local facilities like Vanderbilt or TriStar can exceed $30,000.

Lost Wages

The average hospital stay lasts 4–5 days. For Nashville's self-employed musicians, gig workers, and independent consultants, being hospitalized means facing thousands in medical bills while earning zero income.

Financial Stress

Because of these gaps, 62% of Nashville families report difficulty paying their medical bills.

How Hospital Indemnity Insurance Protects You

A hospital indemnity plan is a supplemental policy designed to sit alongside your existing health insurance to cover these financial gaps. It provides a safety net so an illness or accident doesn't derail your finances.

Cash Paid Directly to You

Unlike traditional health insurance that pays medical providers, this plan pays direct cash benefits to you. Use it for deductibles, co-pays, rent, childcare, groceries, or to make up for lost wages while you recover.

No Network Restrictions

Use your coverage at Vanderbilt University Medical Center, TriStar Centennial, Saint Thomas Midtown, or any other hospital in Middle Tennessee — no network limits or referral requirements.

Immediate First-Day Benefits

Most plans have no deductible or waiting period, meaning you begin receiving your cash benefit from your very first day in the hospital.

What About Medicare?

If you're over 65 or qualify early, hospital indemnity is also a valuable tool to supplement Medicare. Under Medicare Part A, you must pay a deductible before coverage begins, and for extended stays you face steep coinsurance: $371 per day for days 61–90, and $742 per day for days 91 and beyond (using the Part A schedule). A hospital indemnity plan helps offset these daily copays and deductibles so you aren't left holding the bill.
